## What is Health Ledger?

Health Ledger keeps measurements, workouts, medicines and reports on the server you choose. Use the web journal or connect the Android app to the same account.

[Self-host Health Ledger and connect Android](/docs/android-self-hosting) walks through deployment, sign-in, daily sync and importing older Samsung records. For local development, see [Getting started](/docs/getting-started).
